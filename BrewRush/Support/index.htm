How to Play

What do I do?

Customers order drinks and each order appears as a receipt. Drag or swipe each receipt onto the matching pad: hot drinks go to the left pad, cold drinks go to the right pad.

How does the game end?

Every receipt has a timer. The rush ends if a receipt's timer runs out or if you sort a drink onto the wrong pad.

How is my score counted?

Each correctly sorted drink is one cup served. Your best run is saved as your personal best.

Leaderboard & Game Center

My score isn't on the leaderboard.

Scores are posted through Apple's Game Center. Make sure you're signed in under iOS Settings → Game Center. If you scored while offline or signed out, your best is saved on your device and sent the next time you open Brew Rush while signed in and online. New scores can take a minute to appear.

How do I sign out of Game Center?

Game Center is managed by iOS. Go to iOS Settings → Game Center. Brew Rush keeps working offline when you're signed out.

Ads & Privacy

Why are there ads?

Brew Rush is free. A banner ad appears only on the game-over screen and never during play.

How do I change my ad or tracking choices?

Tracking permission is in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you're in a region that requires ad consent (such as the EU or UK), you can change it in the game's Settings under Ad privacy.

See the Privacy Policy for details.

Settings & Your Data

Can I reset my best score?

No. Your best is your record. When you're signed in to Game Center, it's the best score on the leaderboard, which can't be lowered. Deleting the app clears the best saved on this device.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Your personal best and settings are stored only on your device, so deleting the app removes them. Scores already posted to Game Center stay on the leaderboard.

Requirements

Brew Rush runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later.
