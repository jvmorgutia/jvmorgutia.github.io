Brew Rush

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Overview

Brew Rush is an arcade coffee shop game for iPhone and iPad created by Jesse Morgutia. This policy explains what information the app uses, who receives it, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, contact us at any time.

Information We Collect

Brew Rush has no accounts and no sign-up, and we do not run any servers that receive data from the app. The only data the app sends is described below: your score to Apple's Game Center, and advertising-related data to Google AdMob.

Your personal best score and your sound and haptics settings are saved only on your device. You can clear your personal best at any time with Reset Best in the game's Settings, and deleting the app deletes all of them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Brew Rush sends your score to Apple's Game Center leaderboard and shows your global rank. Your Game Center nickname and score are visible to other players on the leaderboard.

Game Center is provided by Apple and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Game Center & Privacy. If you are not signed in to Game Center, no scores are sent to Game Center.

Advertising

Brew Rush is free and shows a banner ad on the game-over screen. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud and abuse, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • information about ads shown and your interactions with them
  • diagnostic information about the app and ad performance

Google and its partners may use device identifiers and similar technologies to show ads based on your use of this and other apps. This information goes to Google, not to us. Google's use of it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Tracking and Your Ad Choices

Brew Rush asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used to show more relevant ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not use that identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you are in a region where privacy law requires consent for ads (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland), Brew Rush shows Google's consent message before any ads load. If you decline, you may see limited or non-personalized ads. You can review or change your choice at any time in the game's Settings under Privacy Choices (shown where your region requires it).

Sharing Your Score

When you tap the share button on the game-over screen, Brew Rush creates an image of your score on your device. It is only sent where you choose to send it, using the standard iOS share sheet.

Analytics

Brew Rush does not include any analytics or crash-reporting software of our own. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports.

Children's Privacy

Brew Rush is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Retention and Your Rights

We do not keep any personal information about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete.

  • Game Center: scores are kept by Apple. You can manage your Game Center profile in iOS Settings → Game Center, and request access to or deletion of your Apple data at privacy.apple.com.
  • Advertising: Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policy. You can limit it with the ad choices above or through Google's My Ad Center.
  • On your device: use Reset Best in Settings, or delete the app.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
