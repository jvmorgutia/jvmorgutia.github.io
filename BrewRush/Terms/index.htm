Scores and Game Center

Brew Rush always saves your best score on your device. If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best score is also posted to the Brew Rush leaderboard, where other players can see your Game Center nickname and score.

If you play while offline or signed out, your best score is kept on your device and sent to the leaderboard the next time you open Brew Rush while signed in and online. When you're signed in, the best score shown in the game is the higher of your device's best and your leaderboard best. Leaderboard scores can't be lowered or reset.
